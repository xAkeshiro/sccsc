Team Leader

Team Leader Position

As a Team Leader, you’ll be the heart of the program, keeping students safe, engaged, and inspired while leading activities that make learning fun. You’ll play a key role in shaping positive experiences and building connections with your communities.

At the Sacramento Chinese Community Service Center (SCCSC), we believe in teamwork, creativity, and making a difference every day. If you’re passionate about working with youth and ready to bring energy to after-school hours, we’d love to have you on our team!

Apply Now
Job Types: Part-time, Contract Pay Range: $17-19.50 / hour
Job Types: Part-time, Contract
Pay Range: $17-19.50 / hour

Core Essential Functions

As a Team Leader, you will:

  • Maintain a safe and supportive environment- both physically and emotionally, for all students.
  • Manage a classroom of up to 20 students, promoting positive behavior and keeping youth actively engaged.
  • Plan and lead age-appropriate activities that support social, emotional, and academic growth, including homework help, enrichment, and recreation.
  • Create and submit lesson plans that align with program goals.
  • Mentor and guide students, encouraging positive behavior and character development.
  • Collaborate with fellow Team Leaders and school staff to ensure smooth program operations.
  • Report any accidents, safety issues, or concerns promptly to the Program Manager.
  • Demonstrate professionalism by treating all staff equitably, maintaining consistent attendance, and upholding the Center’s values.
  • Complete basic cleanup tasks related to classroom and cafeteria use.
  • Participate in staff meetings and professional development as required.

Job Specifications

General Requirements

  • Must be at least 18 years old
  • Valid TB clearance and DOJ/FBI background check
  • Strong interpersonal, communication, and organizational skills
  • Ability to fluently speak, read, write, and comprehend English

Education

  • Required: High School Diploma or equivalent
  • Preferred: 48 units of undergraduate coursework from an accredited college/university OR a Para-Educator Certificate (may vary by school district)
  • Highly Desirable: Associate’s degree or higher in Child Development, Education, Social Services, Psychology, or a related field

Experience (Highly Preferred)

  • At least 1 year of experience working with youth in educational or developmental settings, such as:
  • Classroom instruction or academic tutoring
  • Coaching sports or recreation programs
  • Youth counseling or leadership in summer camps, clubs, or education-based organizations

Physical Demands

  • Ability to stand for extended periods of time
  • Frequent walking throughout the shift
  • Occasionally lift up to 35 pounds
